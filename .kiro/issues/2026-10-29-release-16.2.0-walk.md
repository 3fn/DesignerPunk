## Lessons for Spec 131 (the flow as written vs as run)

1. **Ratify-then-walk is inverted in practice.** Item 1 runs the paste "once the bump is ratified"; the walk record's filename, Trigger, grep `V` and the delegate path need the version. This walk ran on a proposed version, and a different ratification re-cuts it.
2. **`issues-owned.sh` per-seat counts are not the walk's size.** They sum to 73 over 43 records (multi-owner and named-on lines). A brief that sizes the walk from them over-counts by 70%.
3. **Lapse headings owed at S have no assigned seat.** 21 of 24 at 16.1.0's S went unrecorded for three days; the rule counts headings, so the second lapses here would have been invisible.
4. **Rule-11 escalations have no delivery form.** Nine went to Peter at 16.1.0's S on paper; none has an answer on record. This S projects 31. A per-release escalation batch (the floor batch's form) is the missing piece; so is reading the rule's "an ancestor … re-ruled it" (literally, one re-rule makes a record immune forever; Stacy's recount reads the re-rule that set the firing token as not discharging it, and the rule does not say so).
5. **Fixing PRs leave records open.** #452 (P0 left at the root six days as "RESOLVED-pending-merge"), #534 (my own), and the walk record of 16.1.0 itself (still at the root after phase 1) — rule 5's "ideally in the PR" is not met by habit.
6. **Two column-0 Trigger lines in one record** (the trio record L6, L57) double-count in the grep and are `issues-owned.sh`'s stated known gap.
7. **RS-38's input lives outside git.** The `**Straggler sweep**:` line is read from PR bodies only; three of the four PRs' squash messages lack it, so a session without `gh` cannot perform the read.
8. **The delegate's store reach depends on where sessions ran.** Scratchpad-worktree sessions are outside the default store set; the brief has to say so.
9. **No `[Unreleased]` entries accrue.** Shipped-surface PRs (#517, #535, #564) add no CHANGELOG line, so the release author reconstructs them; step 2 assumes a section that does not exist.
10. **The shipped surface is wider than a summary.** The orchestrator's brief listed steering, charters and `src/`; `files[]` also carries 8 changed `governance/` docs and `token-index/` (#517's three files). The flow's "authoritative list: `files[]`" caught it.

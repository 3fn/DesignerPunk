## How to read this record

- **Rows are records, not verdicts.** Each row quotes what its owner has on record. Where an owner has no proposal for this release, the row says `owner's proposal: none on record — [@OWNER]`, and the owner writes one before Peter rules.

Each floor-class row instead carries the M7 form: **fixed here** (the PR, or `no PR`) / **waive requires Peter's words** (quoted, dated, from outside this diff) / **disclosed at the reader's meeting point?** (M7: a CHANGELOG line does not reach an agent reader).

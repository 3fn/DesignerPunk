## 0. The outline in one screen (for Peter)

- **Your goal, in your words** (§ 1): adopters use DesignerPunk's native components as a package dependency, build more with DesignerPunk, and "the components are functional, and will successfully render, in the app as described."
- **Where we are at `8553499b1`**: Spec 129 closed. The generated token files compile on iOS and Android, and our component source compiles against them except for pinned errors (49 iOS, 93 Android, VERIFIED Thurgood by counting `harness/expected/*.tsv`). No app is built, nothing is linked, no component is rendered and no native contract is tested. "Do not start a native product" is still printed.
- **What this spec does**: takes the components from "compiles in our checks" to "builds, links, renders and behaves as described in an adopter's own app", one family at a time. The first family is the Chip family plus Icon-Base plus one adopter-built component.
- **What this round asks of you** (§ 5.1): the definition of done for 132 (D4–D10, with one wording fix to D8), four plan decisions (8–11), the iOS minimum again, when the support claim ships and what it promises for families not yet ready, and up to four new items the review raised (a token review, two possible changes to D5, and how 132's CI lanes get their grants). Each has options and no pick.
- **The probes**: Kenya's iOS spike ran (the tools work on iOS 26.0; iOS 17 was not run, and needs your go). Data's Android probe is unblocked: you merged its grant, #878.
- **What the review found that changes the plan**: the first family is not as cheap as it looked (it carries the blend repair and two accessibility costs, § 2); our components compile as **one** module in an adopter's app, so one broken family can stop the first family's app from building (T-14); and every 132 CI lane needs a `.github/**` grant, which the plan never costed (T-15).

## 4. Preconditions, and records 132 receives

### 4.1 Preconditions before an adopter component's tokens can be trusted

- **#870** — `.kiro/issues/2026-11-27-ios-component-token-type-map-breaks-six-families.md` (Ada, P1, on PR #870, not yet on `main`). A component token in the colour, fontWeight, fontFamily, duration, easing or scale family makes `generate` emit Swift or Kotlin that is wrong or does not compile, and `generate` says nothing. One bad token fails the whole `ComponentTokens` module, and every DesignerPunk component in the consumer's target fails with it.
  - **Why it is 132's precondition** (Kenya's point): D10's adopter component carries its own `defineComponentTokens` file, and WP6 teaches adopters to write more. Both reach this defect on **both** platforms.
  - **The interim rule for D10 until it lands** (corrected in R1 by Ada and Data; on Android it is REASONING from the emitted declarations, not compiled, because neither machine has a JDK):
    - spacing, sizing, radius, fontSize, borderWidth and tapArea, by value or by reference;
    - opacity, lineHeight, density, letterSpacing, blend and breakpoint **by reference only**: on Android their value path emits a bare `Double` or `Int` where the primitive is a `Float` or `TextUnit`;
    - none of colour, fontWeight, fontFamily, duration, easing or scale;
    - and the adopter journey teaches no more than this.
    - Ada adds the Android value-path finding to #870's record, as her act.
  - **Its colour fork is Peter's, in that record** (refuse, staged; or allow mode-aware as a new spec). This outline does not re-ask it. Only if (ii) is picked does S-2 apply.
- **#873** — `.kiro/issues/2026-11-27-product-token-swift-emitter-colour-types.md` (Ada, P1, on PR #873, not yet on `main`). The two static product-colour forms that Product-Token-Governance teaches do not compile on iOS.
  - **Why it bears on 132**: #870's fork (i) sends component authors to product tokens, so (i) is only as good as #873's fix (Ada's counter in #870). D4's adopter theme and D10 use no static product colour on iOS until #873 lands; Leonardo's adopter theme recolours through the semantic-override path instead (whether that path reaches #873's emitter is Ada's to say).

- **#872** (Ada; added in R1) — the CORRECTION of `governance/Token-Governance.md` L160 and L556 and `governance/Token-Quick-Reference.md` L224, which taught that component tokens take "numeric families only".

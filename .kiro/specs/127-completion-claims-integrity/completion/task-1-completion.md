## Lessons learned

**The instrument defect is the finding worth carrying.** Every substantive number in this unit's ballot was correct, and its final verification instrument was broken the whole time: the straggler sweep's `--exclude-dir` flags sat after `--`, so they never bound. It read `.git/COMMIT_EDITMSG` — a file that changes on every commit — which means the ballot's own gate would have returned a different answer each time it ran. **A measure about instruments that report green while verifying nothing shipped with one**, and an external pass found it.

**The root cause is the lesson this law writes down.** § 5's reasoning (worktrees fall outside `.kiro/specs/*/tasks.md`) is correct for § 5 and was carried into § 8, whose commands walk the whole tree. Verified-in-one-section, assumed-in-another — the same shape as the flag-discipline rule now at `Process-Spec-Planning.md:533`: *a transcriber's confidence is an inverse signal.* It arrived on the ballot that writes it.

**Count-differencing does not survive a moving corpus.** Predicted sweep deltas were computed against a tree that did not contain the ballot, then re-measured against one that did, then again after the required reviewer's file landed citing 13 of the 17 swept strings. **The reviewer's own first-pass finding fell to the same error and she corrected it visibly against herself.** The form is retired in favour of per-hit, per-file classification.

**Correct actions with no record are indistinguishable from incorrect ones.** Four application-time adaptations were made; all four were right on the merits and confirmed as such; none was recorded until an external pass looked. There was no completion doc for 1.2 or 1.3 to hold them. *"The applier judged well"* is not a record — ballot § 7.10 is.

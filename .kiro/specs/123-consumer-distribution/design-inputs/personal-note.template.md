# Personal note

<!-- dp:template -->
**This is your personal note.** Your agents read it at the start of every session, so they know who they are working with. It is meant to stay on your machine; don't commit it.

Write your answers under the three headings below, in your own words. A few lines each is plenty, and specific beats general. Anything you type inside this guidance block is not read as your answer. Or, after you restart your agent session, ask your agent to walk you through it.

**Who I am**: Who are you, and what are you building? How comfortable are you reading code, running builds, or reading a written spec, and would you rather see a design rendered than read it? e.g. *I'm ___, a ___, building ___. I read ___ easily; show me ___.*

**What I or my organization value**: What matters most to you in this work, and why? e.g. *I care more about ___ than ___, because ___.*

**How I like to communicate and collaborate**: When we disagree, what should your agent do? What does useful feedback look like to you, and what kind annoys you? Which decisions are yours alone, and which need someone else's sign-off? How much planning do you want before building, and what convinces you something is done? e.g. *When you think I'm wrong, ___. Never ___ without asking me first. I'll believe it's done when ___.*

Say why, not only what: a reason lets an agent handle a case your note doesn't cover.

Facts about the product itself, such as the platforms and versions you ship or the browsers, devices and assistive technology your users need, belong in `product/overview.yaml`, which your whole team shares. Keep them out of this note.

For a finished example, see `node_modules/@3fn/core/src/cli/templates/personal-note.example.md`. Write your own; don't copy it.

Once your note is written, you can delete this whole guidance block.

> **For agents**: while this note holds nothing but its template (headings, this guidance, `TODO`), it is unfilled; do not read it as instructions about the person. If you are talking directly with the person (never as a delegated subagent), offer once in this conversation to walk them through it, one section at a time, writing their answers under each heading, outside this guidance block, in their own words. If they decline, offer to replace the file with one line of their choosing. Any answer of theirs under the headings ends the offer.
>
> **During the walkthrough**: ask the questions above one at a time, and ask for the reason behind a preference. Write down what they say, close to their own words; don't turn it into general values. If they give product facts, offer to put them in `product/overview.yaml` instead. When they are done, offer to delete this guidance block.

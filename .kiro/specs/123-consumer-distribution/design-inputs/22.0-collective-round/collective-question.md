# The one question to the agent collective (22.0)

**Who**: all eight agents (Ada, Lina, Thurgood, Leonardo, Sparky, Kenya, Data, Stacy). Each is asked once, read-only, in its own seat, in parallel. Each gets this question plus the draft `personal-note.template.md`.

**Caveat**: these are DesignerPunk's own seats. In a consumer repo the readers are the consumer renderings of the same eight, so the answers stand in for them. They are a proxy, not a measurement.

---

> **Question.** At the start of every session you read a personal note from the human you work with. Name the **one** thing that note could tell you that would most change what you actually do in your domain. Leave out anything you would assume anyway, such as "values quality" or "likes collaboration".
>
> Answer in exactly this form, **under 80 words in total**:
> 1. **I'd want to know**: … (15 words or fewer)
> 2. **What I'd do differently because of it**: … (a concrete behaviour, 20 words or fewer)
> 3. **Heading it belongs under**: Who I am / What I or my organization value / How I like to communicate and collaborate / none fits
> 4. **Does the attached draft draw it out?** yes — quote the question that does / no — the question you'd add (15 words or fewer)
>
> One item only. If you have two, pick the one with the bigger behaviour change.

---

**How the answers are used** (input, not a ruling):
- Leonardo tabulates them by heading.
- A "no" in item 4 that two or more agents share becomes a candidate question in the guidance block.
- A single "no" is weighed, not automatically added, because the block must stay short: it loads every session until it is deleted.
- An answer in item 3 of "none fits" from two or more agents is raised with Peter as a possible fourth slot. Adding a slot changes the template, but not the detection rule.
- The final bytes are Leonardo's. Peter sees the tally alongside the example.

**Record conventions** (stated in each file's header, so a reader or a checker can decide compliance without asking the author):
- **Unit keys** are Task 10's partition anchors, verbatim, including the long heading-derived slugs. C16's example key `#the-owed-set-pipeline` was shorthand; the real anchor is longer.
- **`canonicalHash`** = `sha256:` + hex SHA-256 over the unit's exact UTF-8 bytes, as `partition(splitFrontmatter(<source>).body)` returns them. **Task 13's freshness sweep must hash the same bytes.**
- **Item `text`** is the complete operative text and a verbatim substring of the unit.
  - Headings are labels, never items (clause (c)).
  - List markers and leading indentation are not part of an item's text.
  - Multi-line items (the owed-set script stages, the Step 4 layout groups) keep their internal bytes.
- **`kind`** is one of 11.6.2's five: `obligation | step | member | route | command`.
- **Confirmation paths**: `canonical/profiles/consumer/confirmations/<source-stem>.md#<unit-anchor>`.

**Proposed confirmation-note convention, for the confirmers** (a proposal; confirmers may amend it):
- One file per source stem. For each unit, a level-2 heading whose text is the unit anchor in backticks, e.g. `` ## `#audit-checklist` ``.
- Under it:
  - `confirmer: <seat>`;
  - `canonicalHash: <same as the record>`;
  - `items: <the ids confirmed>`, or `items: none`;
  - the date.
- **If the owner narrows or widens a set**, the owner edits the operative-set record in their own commit and confirms the edited set. 11.6.5d puts fixing the set in the owner's seat; the drafter never re-edits a confirmed set.
- This makes C16's "internal consistency with the record (item ids, hashes)" checkable.

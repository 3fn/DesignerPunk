## Why it matters

The package ships `governance/` whole, and 18 of its 83 docs name DesignerPunk's own authorities. The banners tell a consumer's agent, at the top of each, what is worked example and what transfers; the predicate makes the set re-runnable instead of a hand-kept list; the guard makes presence checkable and states its own denominator. It cannot show that a banner is read (Req 20.10).
